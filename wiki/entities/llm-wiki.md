# Llm Wiki

The pattern this project implements: raw sources stay untouched and an LLM keeps a linked markdown wiki up to date. Proposed by [[Andrej Karpathy]].

## Karpathy Llm Wiki Concept

> Mentioned 2x
>
> Three layers (raw sources, wiki, schema) and three operations (ingest, query, lint).

[[Karpathy Llm Wiki Concept]]

## Retrieval Augmented Generation

> Mentioned 1x
>
> Addresses RAG limitations by keeping knowledge pre-compiled instead of re-deriving it from chunks on every query.
